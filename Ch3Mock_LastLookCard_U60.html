1. Why animals reproduce

  • Living things reproduce to ensure the continuity of their kind.
  • Overhunting can make a kind extinct.

2. How a baby begins

  • Ovaries release eggs; testes make sperm.
  • Fertilisation happens inside or outside the female's body.
  • The sperm's nucleus fuses with the egg's nucleus.
  • A human baby grows in the womb for about nine months.

3. Heredity

  • Heredity is the passing on of characteristics from parents to offspring.
  • Each of us has one set from the father and one set from the mother.
  • A trait can skip a generation.
  • Gregor Mendel studied pea plants.
