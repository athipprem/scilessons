Two Steps to Any Answer

The race showed it. Here is how to work it out without a race.
  1. What share of the cells divide today? A loop of N days means 1 in N cells divides each day.
  2. Multiply by how many cells there are.
Example: 200 cells, 2-day loop: 200 ÷ 2 = 100 new cells. 60 cells, 1-day loop: 60 new cells. The big slow patch wins. So remember: fast is not the same as many.
It also explains the Photo: with a 4-day loop, only about 1 in 4 cells divides on any day, so a photo catches just a few.
