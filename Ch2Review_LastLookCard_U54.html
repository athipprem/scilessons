1. Plant cell and animal cell

  • The cell is the basic unit of all living things.
  • The nucleus controls the cell. The cell membrane controls what moves in and out. The cytoplasm is the jelly where the work happens.
  • Plant only: cell wall (stiff, made of cellulose, gives shape and support) · large vacuole (holds cell sap; full means firm/turgid) · chloroplasts (make food using light).

2. The microscope

  • Parts: eyepiece, objective lens, stage, light source.
  • Total magnification = eyepiece × objective. Real size = picture size ÷ total magnification.
  • Start on low power: you see more of the slide, so the specimen is easy to find.
  • Making a slide: thin specimen, a drop of stain, coverslip lowered at an angle.
  • Osmosis: water moves in from where there is more water to where there is less.

Unicellular = made of one cell (amoeba, bacteria, yeast). Multicellular = made of many cells. Yeast reproduces by budding: the bud pinches off.

5. Cell division

Grow→Copy chromosomes→Split→two identical cells
  • Cells divide for growth and repair, and to replace old cells.
  • Each round the number of cells doubles: 1 → 2 → 4 → 8. Count the rounds first: rounds = time ÷ gap.
Calc check: (1) say what the number means (“once every 3 days” = share by 3; “×400 bigger” = the real one is smaller, so divide) · (2) write the step · (3) size check: does it make sense?
