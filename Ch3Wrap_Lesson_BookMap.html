Why Do Living Things Reproduce?

Why it matters: every animal you know exists because its parents reproduced.
Some animals have one baby. Some have millions of eggs. All of them reproduce to ensure the continuity of their kind.

Why Do Animals Become Extinct?

1 Overhunting. The Tasmanian tiger was hunted too much.
2 Destroying their homes. Grassland or reefs are lost, and so are the animals living there.
3 Disease. A sickness can spread through a whole kind.
Three causes: overhunting, destroying their homes, disease. For some, nobody knows why. The dinosaurs died out about 65 million years ago, maybe from an asteroid.

Sperm, Egg and the Human Organs

Most animals reproduce when a male sperm and a female egg meet. In humans, ovaries release eggs; testes make sperm. They start to work when the body matures.

Heredity

Heredity is the passing on of characteristics from parents to offspring. These are your traits:
Tongue rolling. Try it!
Earlobes
Eye, hair, skin colour
Height, partly
Genes carry the information. You have two sets of genes: one set from the father, one set from the mother. Gregor Mendel grew pea plants and found a trait can skip a generation and come back. A dominant trait is the one you see.
Heredity is why you look a bit like your parents. Heredity works through genes.
