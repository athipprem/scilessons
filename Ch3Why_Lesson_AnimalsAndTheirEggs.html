Trap-Recognition Drill #18

Watch for ALL, ALWAYS, NEVER. A claim with one of these words is often too extreme.

“An animal that gives NO care to its young ALWAYS dies out.” True or false?

Explain It: Full Marks

A full answer has three linked parts: WHAT happens, WHY (because…), SO what (so…).

👀 Watch me

Why does an ocean sunfish lay hundreds of millions of eggs?
WHAT: It lays a huge number of eggs and leaves them.
WHY: …because most never grow up: they are eaten or lost at sea.
SO: …so a few still survive to replace the parents, which keeps the species going.

⭐ On your own

Why does a lizard’s egg laid in dry sand need a tough skin?
Model: Sand is dry, so water could escape from the egg. The tough skin keeps the water in, because it stops the egg drying out, so the baby lizard can grow.
