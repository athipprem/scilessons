The Flower Rule

Same answer, as a rule.
A flower has petals, an anther and a stigma. No petals, no anther, no stigma: not a flower. A moss capsule has no petals, no anther and no stigma, so moss has no flowers.

The bananas we eat have no seeds that can grow. The young plant is joined to the parent at the base.

The Sucker Rule

Same answer, as a rule.
Joined to the parent at the base: a sucker. Grew alone from a seed: a seedling. Suckers and underground stems are plant parts, not seeds.

A young plant grows beside an old one, joined at the base. Sucker or seedling?

A tiny plant grows alone from a seed in the soil. Sucker or seedling?
