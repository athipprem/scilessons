Tissue: One New Idea 🐝

Concrete route — look at the honeycomb

Stop 4 on the plant is a TISSUE: many cells of the exact SAME kind, side by side. Compare it with stop 3, the whole root: that's SEVERAL DIFFERENT tissues working together as ONE lump. Tissue = same kind of cell, repeated. Organ = different tissues, teamed up.

Key idea: "many of the same" vs. "several different working as a team" is the whole difference between a tissue and the organ built from it.

Click each button. Same plant — only the highlight changes.
Key idea: one lump you could pick up on its own is an ORGAN. Several organs as a team is an ORGAN SYSTEM. Same rule as Unit 49 — now with a picture under it.

Fixing One Wrong Idea 🥔

A potato is a swollen underground STEM — it belongs to the shoot system, not the root system.
A tuber grows from the STEM, underground — it stores food, but it is one lump connected to the shoot, not to the roots. So: a tuber is an ORGAN (part of the shoot system), never an organ system.
