Water World

Many fish let go of eggs and sperm straight into the water. Watch the sperm. What do they need?
The sperm needed water. Fertilisation outside the body only works in water.
Why it matters: a land animal has no pond to use. It needs another way.

The Land Card

Same pond, as steps.
  1. Water or land? Where do the parents live?
  2. Water: sperm swim through water, so fertilisation can be outside the body.
  3. Land: sperm and eggs would dry out on land, so fertilisation is inside. Then either a shelled egg or the embryo grows inside the mother.

Inside the Mother

After fertilisation inside, a mammal’s young starts as an embryo. Press each stage.
The embryo grows inside the mother: warm, fed through her blood, and safe from predators. The safe space is called the womb.

⭐ On your own, typed

Why must a crocodile’s eggs have shells?
Model: Crocodiles lay their eggs on land, so the eggs could dry out. The shell keeps the water in, because it stops the egg drying out, so the baby crocodile can grow.
