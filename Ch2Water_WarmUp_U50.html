Two quick refreshers from Chemistry Unit 40 — separating mixtures. This is unrelated to today's plant topic; it's just keeping an older skill sharp.

1. A bowl holds water with sand mixed in, but nothing dissolved. Which method separates the sand out fastest?

✔ Correct — sand is solid pieces, too big to pass through, with nothing dissolved.
✘ Re-think it: is the sand DISSOLVED, or just mixed in as solid pieces?

2. A classmate claims: "Evaporating NEVER works for recovering a dissolved solid, no matter what it is." Is this true?

✔ Correct — evaporating the water away is exactly how you get a dissolved solid (like salt) back out.
✘ Re-think it: if you boil away all the water, what's left behind in the bowl?
