Quick Warm-Up 🔁

This is an UNGRADED refresher pulling from Unit 12 — material origins — content you haven't seen tested in a while. Just to keep it fresh.

Warm-up. A leather belt is made from a cow's hide (skin). What is leather's ORIGIN group?

✔ Correct — leather comes from an animal's hide, so its origin group is ANIMAL, even though it doesn't look like the cow it came from anymore.
✘ Re-think it: ask what the material actually came from before it was shaped — a cow's hide is part of an animal, not a plant or a factory-made chemical.

Warm-up. Glass is made by melting sand at a very high temperature. What is glass's ORIGIN group?

✔ Correct — sand comes from the ground, so glass's origin group is GROUND/MINERAL. Melting and shaping it doesn't change where it originally came from.
✘ Re-think it: the origin group is about where the raw material came from BEFORE it was processed — sand is a ground/mineral material, not a factory-built chemical like plastic.

Quick Recap: Earth's Two Journeys 🔁

From Unit 7: Earth SPINS on its axis once every 24 hours — that's ROTATION, and it causes day and night. Earth also ORBITS the Sun once every 365 days — that's REVOLUTION, and it's what a "year" means.
Today we go further: what IS the Sun that Earth orbits, and what IS the Earth that's doing all this spinning and orbiting?

The Sun ☀️

The Sun is a STAR — a giant ball of super-hot gases that makes its own light and heat.
The real size difference is enormous — about 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun. It only looks small in the sky because it's so far away (about 150 million km).
Key idea: the Sun is made almost entirely of two gases — hydrogen and helium — packed so tightly and hotly together that it glows and gives off huge amounts of energy as light and heat. It is the SOURCE of almost all the energy on Earth.
Key idea: the Sun is a STAR, not a planet. Stars make their own light; planets (like Earth) only reflect light from a star.

The Earth 🌍

Earth is made of three main layers: a thin rocky CRUST, a hot rocky MANTLE, and a mostly-metal CORE at the very centre.
The crust is what we walk on — it's the THINNEST layer by far. The mantle is thick, hot, and slowly moving rock. The core, at the very centre, is mostly iron and nickel, and it's extremely hot.
Key idea — why Earth supports life: Earth sits at just the right distance from the Sun (not too hot, not too cold), has an ATMOSPHERE (a layer of gases, including oxygen, wrapped around the planet), and has liquid WATER on its surface. All three together are why living things can survive here.
