Purple is dominant (strong). White is recessive (hidden). Mendel found this in peas.

Card Rules

  1. A dominant (strong) card shows. The strong card hides the hidden one.
  2. A recessive (hidden) trait needs two hidden cards to show.

Tall is dominant (strong). One tall card + one short card. How tall is the plant?

A Family Tree

Plain is dominant; spots are recessive. Grandma has spots. Neither parent does. One grandchild does. Tap each plain parent.
Each plain parent carries a recessive spots card. The spotted baby got both: two hidden cards to show. Hidden traits can skip a generation.
