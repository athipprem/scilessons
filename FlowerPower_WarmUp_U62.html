Rep 4: Written Answers Are Back

How to write a 2-mark answer
1. Copy the question's words to start. 2. Add because. 3. Give two ideas. 4. Tick: did I answer WHY?

Explain why most fish lay thousands of eggs. Start: Most fish lay thousands of eggs because…

Model: Most fish lay thousands of eggs because the eggs are left alone and many are eaten. So only a few young survive, but with so many laid, enough survive.
