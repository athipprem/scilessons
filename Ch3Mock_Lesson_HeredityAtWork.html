Heredity: the Book on One Screen

Why it matters: heredity is the big idea in the second half of Chapter 3, and the school test asks about it.
Traits are things about you that can be passed on: eye colour, hair colour, skin colour, earlobes, tongue rolling. Heredity is the passing on of characteristics from parents to offspring.
Two earlobe traits, from the book
The instructions for each trait are in your genes. Each of us has two sets of genes: one set from the father and one set from the mother.
Gregor Mendel studied pea plants
Gregor Mendel grew pea plants and watched their flower colours. He found out how traits are passed on.
A trait can skip a generation
The white flower is in the grandparent, hides in the parent and comes back in the child. A trait can skip a generation.

Where do your two sets of genes come from?

Inherited or From Surroundings?

Tap a card, then tap its bin.
Remember: surroundings can change you, but only genes are passed on. A dye, a healed bone or a trained trick is not passed on to children.

Selective Breeding: the Hen Farm

A farmer wants hens that lay more eggs. Selective breeding means: choose the parents with the trait you want, and breed only from them.
Tap the 2 hens with the most eggs, then press Breed.
The bars grow. In selective breeding you choose the parents with the trait you want, and after many generations most chicks have it. That is selective breeding.

After many generations of selective breeding, why do the hens lay more eggs?
