Divide or Multiply? Ask What the Number Means

A number can mean “this is bigger” or “this is shared out”. Slide and watch which way the bar goes.
A ladybird photo, 40 mm long
Photo 40 mm
Real 8 mm
The photo is ×5 bigger, so the real size is smaller. Divide: 40 ÷ 5 = 8 mm.
A plant waters itself once every 4 days
1 day
In 1 day it does only a quarter of its 4-day job. Divide by 4.

The Three-Line Rule

Same idea, as a rule you can use on any question.
  1. Ask: is the real thing bigger or smaller than the number I am holding?
  2. Smaller: divide. Bigger: multiply.
  3. Sense-check: does the size of my answer make sense?
A ladybird photo is 40 mm at ×5. The real size is smaller, so divide: 40 ÷ 5 = 8 mm.
90 cells, each dividing once every 3 days. Each cell does a third of a division a day, so divide: 90 ÷ 3 = 30 a day.
12 cars an hour for 2 hours. The total is bigger, so multiply: 12 × 2 = 24.
