The Butterfly Life Cycle

Why it matters: many young animals look nothing like their parents. A life cycle tells you what comes next.
The butterfly life cycle, from the book
Four stages: egg, larva, pupa, adult. The larva (a caterpillar) looks very different from the adult butterfly. It grows, then changes inside the pupa.

Complete or Incomplete?

Complete: egg, larva, pupa, adult. Incomplete: egg, nymph, adult. A nymph looks like a small adult, but with no wings yet.
A grasshopper nymph and an adult

Grasshopper: it has a nymph

The mosquito has a pupa, so it is complete. The grasshopper has a nymph and no pupa, so it is incomplete. A nymph grows into an adult by getting bigger and growing wings.

Young and Adults Eat Differently

A caterpillar eats leaves. The butterfly sips nectar.
The larva eats leaves. The adult sips nectar from flowers. They need different food, so young and adults do not compete for the same food.
