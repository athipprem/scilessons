Plants With No Seeds

Not every plant makes flowers and seeds. Ferns and moss grow on walls, trees and forest ground, and they make no seeds at all. So how do they make new plants? Knowing plants' tricks helps gardeners and farmers grow more of them.

Ferns and moss have no flowers. They are non-flowering plants. Some non-flowering plants such as ferns and moss reproduce from spores.

Flip the frond. Then tap a dot.
The top of the frond is just green leaflets. Flip it over.
Under a mature fern frond are clusters of spore bags, each holding thousands of tiny spores. If the conditions are right, the spores grow into new ferns. Remember: spore bags on the underside of the frond hold the spores. Spores are tiny and light, so the wind carries them to new places. Spores come from spore bags on the underside, not from flowers.

Suckers

A banana farmer does not plant seeds. A new banana plant simply grows beside the old one.

Suckers are new shoots that grow from buds at the base of the parent plant's stem. Banana and pineapple plants grow suckers. A sucker grows into a new plant.

Plants grown from suckers take a shorter time to grow into adult plants than plants grown from seeds. That is why farmers like suckers.

Underground Stems

Some stems grow under the soil. You have eaten them.

A potato looks like a root, but the parts we eat are stems. In potato and ginger plants, the parts we eat are stems that grow underground. They have buds, and a bud grows into a young plant, as the picture shows. A ginger shoot grows up from its underground stem.

Sort each plant into its box. The card then shows its trick.
Suckers: new shoots from the base. Underground stems: the parts we eat are stems.
Remember: suckers are new shoots that grow from buds at the base. In underground stems, the parts we eat are stems.

New Plants From Leaves

A single leaf can start a new plant.

The bryophyllum grows tiny new shoots along the edges of its leaves. Each new shoot is a plantlet. It falls to the soil and grows into a new plant. African violet and begonia can also reproduce from leaves.

Plants can reproduce from plant parts such as suckers, leaves and underground stems.
